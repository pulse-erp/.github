# Pulse ERP

Welcome to the **Pulse ERP** organization.

We are building a complete and modern **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)** solution to help businesses run their operations from a single, integrated platform.

## What We Build

Our ERP ecosystem is designed to cover core business domains, including:

- Finance and Accounting
- Human Resources and Payroll
- Procurement and Inventory
- Sales and Customer Management
- Operations and Reporting

## Our Focus

- **Scalable architecture** for growing organizations
- **Modular design** for flexible adoption
- **Secure and reliable systems** for business-critical workflows
- **Automation-first approach** to reduce manual effort

## Goal

Deliver a clean, connected, and extensible ERP platform that improves productivity, transparency, and decision-making across every department.
